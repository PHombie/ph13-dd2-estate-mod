================================================================================

                THE PICTURES IN THIS FOLDER: A DEMONSTRATION ONLY

================================================================================

  The PNG files beside this notice are redrawn environment textures for the
  Warrens.

  They were made from the original art of Darkest Dungeon by Red Hook Studios:
  each one keeps the subject, layout and silhouettes of the original picture
  of the same name and repaints it, with an image-generation tool, in a manner
  closer to Darkest Dungeon II.

  ALL RIGHTS TO THE ORIGINAL ART BELONG TO RED HOOK STUDIOS INC.

  These pictures are not Red Hook's work, are not endorsed by Red Hook, and
  are not offered as a replacement for it.  They are here for one purpose
  only: to show that the mod can draw a picture of its own in place of one of
  the original game's, at the original's size, whatever its pixels.  The
  "anything you like" of the mod's LICENSE.txt does not cover them.

  The mod does not need them.  Delete this folder and the mod draws the
  Warrens from the copy of Darkest Dungeon you own, exactly as it draws every
  other place.

  How the mod uses them: a PNG at  assets/<the picture's path in the Darkest
  Dungeon install>  is read instead of the original file.  The build copies
  assets/ beside the plugin.

  If you hold rights in the originals and want these pictures gone, they will
  be removed.

================================================================================
